# 🍏 Apple by the Numbers: Growth, Revenue and Valuation

This Tableau dashboard analyzes **Apple’s financial performance and shareholder value creation over 15 years (2009–2024)**.  
The interactive design allows switching between two perspectives:  

1. **Revenue & Profitability** (how Apple earns and grows)  
2. **Stocks & Shareholder Value** (how shareholders benefit)  

---

## 🔎 Insights  

### 📈 Revenue & Profitability View  
- **Revenue grew ~9x** from 2009 ($42B) to 2024 ($391B).  
- **Net income nearly doubled** between 2020 and 2023, peaking above $100B.  
- **Gross margin expanded from 38% in 2020 to 46% in 2024**, showing stronger pricing power and cost control.  
- **Revenue per employee rose** from ~$1.2M (2009) to ~$2.4M (2024), reflecting efficiency gains.  
- **Debt peaked in 2017** but declined after, while Apple built a stronger cash cushion.  

---

### 💹 Stocks & Shareholder Value View  
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS) climbed from under $1 in 2009 to $6 in 2024**, showing strong earnings growth.  
- **Stock price surged from $6 in 2009 to $243 in 2024** (~40x growth), closely tracking EPS.  
- **Shares outstanding declined after 2015** due to buybacks, boosting per-share value and EPS growth.  
- **Market Cap growth moved in line with EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation & Amortization)**, reinforcing Apple’s long-term value creation.  

---

## 📂 Dataset   
- **Source:** Kaggle  
- **Fields:** Revenue, Gross Profit, Net Income, EPS, Assets, Liabilities, Debt, Cash, Employees, Market Cap, PE ratio.
